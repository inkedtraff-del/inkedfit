FAQ

Do I need a gym or any equipment?

Yes, standard gym equipment is required. The protocol relies on cables, machines and free weights to keep constant tension on the muscle.

What if it doesn’t work for me?

Follow the sets, reps and nutrition consistently and you will see progress. The system is built on the same principles that work for every lifter: progressive overload, enough protein, enough sleep.

What if I don’t know what to do in the gym?

The guide tells you exactly what to do. Every session has prescribed sets, reps and rest times, so there is no guesswork.

Do I need to follow a strict diet?

You get the full nutrition matrix. Food choices are flexible, but tracking your calories and protein is what gets you to your goal.

What if an exercise feels too hard?

Every exercise scales. Adjust the weight to your current strength while keeping the prescribed rep range.
